Women’s Clothing Sizing

Royal Size SA / UK US EU Bust (cm) Waist (cm) Hips (cm)
XS 30 / 6 2 34 78–82 60–64 84–88
S 32 / 8 4 36 83–87 65–69 89–93
M 34 / 10 6 38 88–92 70–74 94–98
L 36 / 12 8 40 93–97 75–79 99–103
XL 38 / 14 10 42 98–102 80–84 104–108

How to measure:
• Bust: Measured around the fullest part of your chest with your arms relaxed.
• Waist: Measured around the narrowest point of your natural waistline.
• Hips: Measured around the fullest part of your hips and rear.
Tip: If you fall between two sizes, choose the larger size for a standard comfortable fit.

Footwear Guidelines: Shoe sizing varies across international regions. Stand straight on a flat surface with your heel against the wall and measure from heel to your longest toe. If you wear thick socks or have wider feet, consider ordering one size up.

Jewellery (Rings) Sizing

Ring Size Inside Diameter (mm) Circumference (mm)
Small 15.7 49.3
Medium 16.5 51.8
Large 17.3 54.4
X-Large 18.1 56.9

Ring Sizing Method:
• Diameter: The inside width across the widest part of a ring that already fits you perfectly.
• Circumference: Wrap a non-stretching string or measuring tape snug around your knuckle or finger base and measure total length in millimeters.
